# Brand OS: Operating System for Brands & Deterministic Content Creation

A complete, MIT-licensed starter kit and production factory that turns Claude Code, Codex, or Antigravity into your personal **Brand Operating System (Brand OS)**. Audience: creators, founders, marketing engineers, B2B consultants, and creative agencies building compounding brand authority.

Start with the `/using-brand-os-skills` guide — it maps the whole system and routes every task. Brand OS personalizes itself to your brand via the `/brand-onboard` wizard. Use `/grill-me` whenever you need to stress-test a raw concept or contrarian angle. Use the resident skill `carousel-batch` inside `Content Manager/carousel-studio/` to produce pixel-perfect **3:4 carousels (1080 × 1440 px)** in 3 decoupled phases, `/script-shortform` to generate high-retention **9:16 shortform video scripts (1080 × 1920 px)**, `/brand-audit` to verify aspect ratios and proof citations, and `/level-up` to turn weekly retention metrics into compounding brand intelligence.

> **Brand OS** bridges the gap between high-level brand strategy and deterministic code execution. Obsidian acts as the cognitive control tower (`vault/`), a deterministic CSS design system guarantees visual standards (`design-system/`), and headless engines (`Content Manager/`) render publication-ready assets with mathematical precision.

## ⚡ Quick Start (5-Minute First-Time Setup)

1. **Clone or extract the repo** to a local folder on your computer.
2. **Open in Obsidian:** Open `vault/` in Obsidian to activate your visual Brand Brain, Kanban (`Board.md`), and Dashboard.
3. **Open in Claude Code, Codex, or Antigravity:**
   - In Claude Code or Antigravity: run `/brand-onboard`.
   - In Codex: type `$` and select `brand-onboard`.
4. **Answer the 7 intake blocks:** Have your brand mission, target audience pain, core offer, and sample copy ready (~15 mins).
5. **Produce your first batch:**
   - Ask: *"Run `/carousel-batch` to produce our first 3:4 carousel batch on our active pillar."*
   - Render the slides deterministically: `node "Content Manager/carousel-studio/capture.cjs"`.
6. **Day 7:** Run `/brand-audit` to verify aspect ratio compliance, proof citations, and safe zones.
7. **Day 14:** Run `/level-up` to capture weekly retention discoveries into `vault/Findings.md`.

## 📐 Canonical Aspect Ratios & Design Rules

- **Social Carousels (Instagram / LinkedIn):** **Proporción 3:4** (`1080 × 1440 px`, rendered at 2× DPI: `2160 × 2880 px`). *Formato 4:5 y 1:1 estrictamente prohibidos.*
- **Vertical Shortform Video (Reels / TikTok / Shorts):** **Proporción 9:16** (`1080 × 1920 px`).
- **Safe Margins:** Minimum 96px padding on all slide edges. Video text overlays constrained between Y=320px and Y=1500px.
- **The 3-Color Rule:** Background (`--surface-page`), Typography (`--text-strong`), Accent (`--accent`). Only **1 single highlighted word** per slide.
- **Zero Text in AI Images:** Background images generated by AI must be 100% clean of letters, logos, or numbers. Typography is deterministically stamped in HTML/CSS.

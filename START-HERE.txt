BRAND OS - START HERE

1. Extract or clone this folder to your computer. Keep every file and folder together, including the hidden .agents and .claude folders.
2. Open the Brand OS folder in Obsidian to visualize your Brand Brain, Content Dashboard, and Strategy frameworks.
3. Open the same Brand OS folder as a project in your AI coding agent (Claude Code, Antigravity, or Codex).
4. Run /brand-onboard (in Claude Code or Antigravity) or select $brand-onboard (in Codex) to trigger the Brand Intake Wizard.
5. Answer the 7 setup blocks. Have your brand mission, core offer, target audience pains, and sample copy ready.
6. Once onboarded, ask: "Based on my active content pillar and proof bank, generate our first 3:4 carousel batch with /carousel-batch."

Skills at your command:
- /brand-onboard     -> Day-1 setup wizard to populate your Brand Brain (vault/Conceptos/).
- /brand-audit       -> Evidence-based system audit (checks 3:4 & 9:16 ratios, 96px safe margins, proof citations, link integrity).
- /carousel-batch    -> Guided 3-phase production engine for weekly 3:4 carousels (1080 x 1440 px).
- /script-shortform  -> 4-movement high-retention 9:16 shortform video script generator with audio cues and visual directions.
- /grill-me          -> Interactive adversarial interview to stress-test ideas, hooks, and contrarian angles before drafting.
- /level-up          -> Weekly calibration skill to review content metrics and append discoveries to Findings.md and Repos.md.

If skills do not appear, verify that you opened the root Brand OS folder (containing AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, and README.md), ensure .claude and .agents are present, and restart your agent.

Format Guarantees:
- Carousels: Canonical 3:4 aspect ratio (1080 x 1440 px) rendered via AI Content Manager/carousel-studio.
- Shortform Video: Canonical 9:16 aspect ratio (1080 x 1920 px) rendered via AI Content Manager/video-studio.
- Backgrounds: AI-generated images are 100% clean of text; typography is stamped deterministically in code.
